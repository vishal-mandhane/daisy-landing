Bolo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Bolo is a translator for Indians travelling abroad. It is owned and run by Solika Ventures Pvt Ltd, a company incorporated in India ("we", "us"). This policy follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. The short version

  • No account, no ads, no analytics. Bolo has no server of ours behind it.
  • What you type, say and photograph is not sent to us. Translation and reading text in photos happen on your phone once a language is downloaded.
  • Until a language is downloaded, Bolo can translate over the internet with MyMemory (section 3). You can turn that off in Settings.
  • What Bolo remembers stays on your phone, and you can delete it.
  • Two things can involve Google: downloading language models, and your phone's speech recognition when it has no offline voice pack (section 3).

2. What stays on your phone

  • Your settings: the app's language, your language, where you're going, whether polite phrases should use a man's or a woman's words, what you eat and your allergies, starred phrases, and the addresses you add for taxi cards.
  • Your conversation: the last 100 lines you translated in Talk, so you can look back at them. Clear them any time in Talk or Settings.
  • Photos you scan: read on the phone, line by line. Bolo doesn't save them to your gallery or keep its own copy; a temporary file may remain in the app's cache until Android clears it.

Nobody else, including us, can see any of this.

3. What leaves your phone, and to whom

  • Language models (Google ML Kit): when you finish setup, choose a new destination or tap Download, your phone downloads that language's translation model (about 30 MB) from Google. Like any download, Google sees the request. Bolo downloads on any connection unless you choose "Download only on Wi-Fi" on the Trip tab.
  • Online translation while a language isn't downloaded (MyMemory, run by Translated srl, Italy): if a language you need isn't on the phone yet, Bolo sends the words to be translated (only those words, with no name, account or device ID) to MyMemory and shows the answer marked "Translated online". MyMemory may keep the text under its own terms and privacy policy. Once the language is downloaded, Bolo stops using it for that language. Turn it off in Settings → "Translate online until a language is downloaded".
  • Speech recognition (your phone's own service, usually Google): Bolo uses Android's speech recogniser. If your phone has the offline speech pack for that language, recognition happens on the phone. If not, Android may send the audio to Google to recognise it, under Google's privacy policy. Bolo does not receive or keep the audio.
  • Speaking aloud: your phone's text-to-speech engine says the words, on the phone.
  • Calls and websites: when you tap Call, your phone's dialler opens with the number; when you tap a website, your browser opens it. Bolo doesn't place calls or see what you do there.

We, Solika Ventures, receive nothing from Bolo.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only while a microphone button is listening.
  • Camera: used only when you tap "Take a photo", through Android's camera.
  • Internet: to download language models, for online translation until a language is downloaded (if on), and for speech recognition when your phone needs it.

5. Your rights and choices

Under the DPDP Act you can see, correct and erase your data. With Bolo it's all on your phone:

  • Change your settings, food rules and addresses in Trip.
  • Erase your conversation in Talk or Settings, and addresses in My addresses.
  • Erase everything: uninstall Bolo, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Bolo → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Remove language models: clearing Bolo's storage or uninstalling removes them.

6. Children

Bolo is made for travellers and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

7. Security

Bolo keeps your data in its private storage on the phone, which other apps can't read. Keep your phone locked.
