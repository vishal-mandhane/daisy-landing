1. What Bolo is

Bolo helps you talk with people abroad: it translates what you say or type, reads text in photos, and has phrases, questions people can answer with a tap, and cards to show.

Translations are made by software on your phone and can be wrong. Short, simple sentences work best. For anything that matters (money, medicine, the law), check the answer another way too.

2. The food check and food cards

  • Bolo looks for words on a menu or packet that don't suit the food rules you chose. It reads words; it can't see the kitchen. A dish can contain things its name doesn't mention.
  • Bolo never confirms that a food is safe for you. When it finds nothing, it says so and tells you to ask.
  • If you have a serious allergy, always confirm with the staff, read the ingredients, and carry your medicine.
  • You decide what to eat. We are not responsible for what you eat or drink.
