Speak anywhere, in your own language.

Bolo is a translator made for Indians travelling abroad. Speak Hindi, English, Marathi, Tamil or more; Bolo speaks Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese, Korean, Chinese, French, Italian, Spanish or German.

  • Face to face on one phone: their half faces them
  • Scan a menu: it marks pork, fish stock, egg, onion, whatever you don't eat
  • Ask and they tap the answer: yes or no, a price, which way
  • A food card in their language that names dashi, fish sauce and gelatine
  • Emergency numbers and the Indian embassy, one tap to call
  • Works without internet. No account, nothing uploaded
