Last updated: 21 September 2026

Dayzi ("Dayzi", "we", "us", "our") is a social events application that helps people discover, create, and join local events. Dayzi is owned and operated by Solika Ventures Pvt Ltd, a company incorporated in India, which is the data controller (and, where applicable, the "data fiduciary" or "business") responsible for your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we process, why, the legal bases we rely on, how long we keep it, who we share it with, how we protect it, and what rights you have. It applies to:

  • the Dayzi mobile application (the "App"); and
  • the Dayzi website at dayzi.live (the "Site"), including our waitlist / early-registration sign-up.

We process your personal data only where we have a valid legal basis. Some processing is necessary to provide Dayzi under our Terms; some is based on your consent; some is required by law; and some rests on our legitimate interests, such as keeping Dayzi safe. Where we rely on consent, we ask for it separately and you may withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use Dayzi.

1. Information We Collect

Fields marked required are needed to create and operate your account. All others are optional and you can add, change, or remove them at any time.

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information (required): first name, last name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), age, username
  • Account information (optional): gender
  • Profile information (optional): profile photo, bio ("about me"), favourite song, favourite show, user vibes, profile prompts, profession, and a link to your Instagram profile
  • Event information: event title, description, category, area/location, date and time, cover image
  • Communications: messages you send in event group chats, event join requests, appeals, bug reports, and support or grievance requests
  • Safety contact (required to use the App, see Section 3B): the name and phone number of one trusted contact. This is stored only on your own device and is never transmitted to us.
  • Face verification (optional): if you choose to verify, we process a selfie (with a gesture for liveness) together with your profile photo (see Section 3)
  • Waitlist (Site): the email address you submit on dayzi.live

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and connection information: device type, manufacturer and model, operating system version, app version and build, device locale, IP address, and general network information
  • Location: your city and country, and how they were derived (device location services, where you grant permission, or a city you select manually). Coordinates saved for your profile or for an event are rounded to roughly one kilometre before storage, so they identify an area and not an address. We do not track your location continuously or in the background. The one exception is the SOS safety feature, which records your exact location at the moment you press it (see Section 3B).
  • Usage data: events viewed, joined, and created; app interactions; screen names; session duration
  • Advertising identifier: our analytics component (Firebase Analytics) collects the Android Advertising ID on Android devices. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell or share it, and run no advertising SDKs. It is disclosed here because it is technically collected.
  • Analytics identity: analytics events are associated with your Dayzi account identifier. Our in-app analytics are therefore not anonymous.
  • Logs and security events: authentication events, access logs, rate-limit and abuse-prevention signals, and other security telemetry
  • Moderation results: the outcome of automated content moderation, content hashes of images and text that have been checked, and verification status including limited records of failed attempts
  • Push notification tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens
  • Crash and performance data: crash logs and performance metrics, via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Motion sensor (on-device only): the App reads the accelerometer so that shaking your phone can open the bug-report tool. This stream stays on your device and is never transmitted.
  • Bug reports: if you submit one, we collect a screenshot of your screen at that moment, your device model and OS, the app version, your locale, and your email address. A screenshot may capture whatever was on screen, including another person's message, so please review it before sending.

1.3 Information From Third Parties

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the basic profile information you authorise, including the provider's user identifier and, where you permit it, your name and email address.

1.4 Information We Deliberately Do Not Collect

  • Your trusted contact's phone number. It stays on your device. We never receive it, store it, or contact that person ourselves.
  • Your own phone number. Dayzi does not require or store one.
  • Continuous or background GPS. Your exact location is recorded only at the moment you press SOS.
  • Payment card or bank details. Dayzi does not currently take payments.
  • Sensitive attributes by inference. We do not use automated systems to infer religion, political views, sexual orientation, caste, health, or ethnicity.

3. AI, Content Moderation, Biometrics, and Automated Processing

These tools assist our safety decisions. They can make mistakes, and they do not by themselves permanently terminate your account without the possibility of human review (Section 3.7).

3.1 Image Moderation

Profile photos and event cover images are analysed by SightEngine to detect inappropriate content such as nudity or violence. Flagged images may be blocked or removed. Where SightEngine is unavailable, Google Gemini may perform the same check as a fallback. We store a hash of images that pass or fail, not the moderation scores.

3.2 Text Moderation

Event names and descriptions, and free text on your profile — your "about me", profile prompt answers, profession and social links — are analysed by Google Gemini API (via Google Cloud) to detect offensive, hateful, or inappropriate language. Profile text is checked after it is saved; text that fails is removed and you are notified.

3.3 Optional AI-Generated Descriptions

An optional feature uses Google Gemini API to draft an event description from details you provide. It runs only when you choose it.

3.4 Face Detection on Profile Photos — this applies to everyone

When you upload a profile photo, it is sent to Amazon Rekognition (Amazon Web Services, region ap-south-1, Mumbai) to check whether it contains a human face. This is a face-detection check — it counts faces. It does not identify you and does not create a faceprint. It runs on every profile photo, including for people who never use face verification. Today it is advisory only and does not block your upload. We keep a record only where no face was found.

3.5 Face Verification — optional, and biometric

Face verification is optional. It confirms you are a real, unique person and reduces fake accounts. It is not a background check and is not a guarantee of anyone's identity, character, or safety.

  • Explicit consent. A facial image used to identify a person is special-category / biometric data. We ask for your explicit consent before processing your selfie and profile photo for this purpose.
  • Who performs the comparison. Your selfie and your profile photo are sent to Amazon Rekognition (AWS, ap-south-1, Mumbai), which compares the two faces and returns a similarity score. Your selfie is then sent to Google Gemini API for a separate liveness check, confirming you performed the requested gesture. Amazon Rekognition, not Google, performs the face match.
  • What we keep. Neither image is stored by Dayzi after the check. We retain your verification status (pass or fail), the date, the method used, and limited audit logs of attempts for security and abuse prevention.
  • One exception. If verification fails and you choose to appeal, the selfie from that attempt is uploaded with your appeal so a person can review it against your profile photo. It is deleted once the appeal is resolved.
  • No secondary use. Face data is never used for advertising, profiling, model training, or any unrelated purpose.
  • Refusal. Declining verification does not block general use of Dayzi, though it may limit specific trust-and-safety features.

3.6 AI and Vendor Data Terms

We use the Google Gemini API as a paid service through a Google Cloud project. Under Google's paid API terms, Google does not use submitted prompts, images, or responses to train or improve its products, and processes that data under its applicable Data Processing Addendum. We do not use unpaid Gemini services for moderation, description generation, or verification.

Amazon Web Services processes images submitted to Amazon Rekognition under the AWS Service Terms and its Data Processing Addendum. Under those terms AWS may, by default, use content submitted to its AI services to improve those services, including for model training. We have opted out. An organisation-wide AI services opt-out policy is in force across our AWS account, so your photos and selfies are not used for service improvement or model training, and AWS has deleted any such content it previously held for that purpose.

SightEngine processes images under its data-processing terms. It trains its models only on media a customer voluntarily submits through its feedback mechanism. We do not use that mechanism, so your images are not used to train SightEngine's models.

3.7 Human Review and Appeals

If your content is removed, restricted, or flagged, or if verification fails, you may request human review at dayzibusiness@gmail.com or through the in-app appeal flow. We do not use AI moderation to infer sensitive attributes.

3B. Safety Check-In (SOS) — please read this carefully

Dayzi includes a safety feature ("SOS") to help you reach one trusted person quickly if an event feels unsafe.

  • Setting up a trusted contact is required to use Dayzi. You cannot finish onboarding without saving one name and one phone number. Whether you ever press SOS is entirely your choice.
  • Your trusted contact stays on your device. The name and number are stored only in your phone's local storage. We do not upload, receive, or store them, and we never contact that person ourselves.
  • How an alert is sent. Pressing SOS opens WhatsApp on your phone with a pre-filled message and a map link. You send it, from your own WhatsApp account. We are not a party to that message. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to it.
  • We do record a safety case. Pressing SOS creates a record on our servers containing: your account, the event you were attending (if any), the time, the status (open, safe, or no response), your device platform and app version, and your device's precise location at that moment, together with its accuracy. Unlike every other location in the App, this one is not rounded, because its purpose is to let someone find you. It contains no phone numbers.
  • Who sees it. Dayzi's safety team can view open cases, including the location, in order to follow up. This is an internal trust-and-safety record. It is not a report to the police or any other authority, and we do not forward it to one as a matter of course. We would disclose it only where Section 4.3 applies.
  • Retention. The precise location is erased 30 days after the SOS was triggered. The case record itself is deleted 90 days after it was triggered. Everything is deleted if you delete your account.
  • Follow-up. After you press SOS we may ask "Are you safe?" in the App and by push notification, and we will ask again before you join or create another event until you answer.
  • SOS is not an emergency service. It alerts a person you chose. It does not contact the police, an ambulance, or any emergency service, and nobody from Dayzi is dispatched to you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number first — for example 112 in India and the EU, 911 in the United States, 999 in the United Kingdom, 000 in Australia.
  • It can fail. SOS depends on your battery, your signal, your device's location permission, WhatsApp being installed and working, you tapping send, and your contact seeing the message. If you close WhatsApp without tapping send, nobody is alerted, even though a case was logged with us. Do not rely on SOS as your only safety plan. Sections 4.5 and 10 of our Terms set out the legal position.

7. Account Deletion — and What Survives It

You can delete your account in the App at Privacy Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing us (see the Delete Account page on dayzi.live). Deletion enters a 30-day grace period, during which logging back in restores your account. After that it is permanent.

Deleted: your profile and photo, events you created, your group chat messages, notifications, group memberships and attendee entries, FCM tokens, SOS cases, and cached data.

Retained, and you should know this before you delete:

  • Safety and moderation records. Reports you filed or that were filed about you, and their aggregates, are kept for the integrity of our safety system and may contain your name.
  • Appeals. Ban appeals and event appeals are retained, and may contain your name and email address.
  • Bug reports. Retained until resolved, and may contain your email address, device details and a screenshot.
  • Voting records from events you attended.
  • Shared content. Messages and event participation records that other people also hold may remain visible where necessary for event history, safety, or integrity.
  • Anything else we must keep by law, or for security, fraud prevention, or the establishment or defence of legal claims.
  • Aggregated, anonymised analytics that no longer identify you.

If you want any retained item removed, contact us and we will assess the request against the exemptions above.
