Thikana Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Thikana helps people who are new to Bengaluru or Mumbai find a room, a PG or a flat without brokers, and a bed for a night near work ("couch stays"). It is owned and run by Solika Ventures Pvt Ltd, a company incorporated in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

By using Thikana you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

1. What we collect

1.1 What you give us

  • Your card: your first name, gender, the office area you work at, your budget, when you want to move in, what you're looking for (room, PG or flat), how you live (veg or not, smoking, drinking, guests, early or late, tidiness, working from home) and an optional line about you.
  • Your phone and WhatsApp number: asked for the first time you ask to visit a place or post one.
  • Your work email, if you verify it: we email it a code. We keep only the company domain (for example "@tcs.com") for the "works at" badge. We do not store the address itself. To stop abuse we keep a one-way hash of it, with no link to you, for 24 hours.
  • Posts: the area, building or society, flat number, rent, deposit and other charges, house rules, whether the owner has agreed (for posts by tenants), a note, and details you add about the people living there (first name, age, work, habits). Only add details about flatmates if they agree.
  • Photos of a place, if you add them to a post: taken with the camera inside the app (Thikana never opens your gallery). Anyone with the link can see a post's photos, so leave out people, faces and documents.
  • Move-in records, if you keep one: your notes and photos of a flat's condition, each with the time it was saved. Only you can see them. Nobody else sees them unless you send the text on WhatsApp yourself.
  • Proof of stay, only if you choose to send one for a review: one photo of a rent agreement's first page or a rent receipt. Please cover ID and bank numbers first. Only a person at Thikana sees it, to check you lived there. We delete it as soon as it's checked, or after 30 days if nobody has.
  • Intros: the note you send when you ask to visit, and the yes or no when someone asks you.
  • Society answers: whether a building allows bachelors, and an optional note.
  • Couch stays, if you host: your couch's area, a landmark near it, what you offer (a sofa, a mattress, a bed in a shared room or a room), who can stay, which nights, arrival and leaving times, what's there, the contribution you ask, house rules and camera photos, with your first name and verified work domain. The exact address goes only to a guest you say yes to.
  • Couch stays, if you ask to stay: the nights, your note, and a short card: your first name, gender, verified work domain, and your stays so far (how many, the average rating hosts gave you, and late cancellations).
  • Stay ratings: after a stay, the guest rates the couch and the host rates the guest (1 to 5, "would host or stay again", an optional note).
  • Reviews of places you lived in: dates, whether you got your deposit back, what was deducted, what went wrong, and anything you write in your own words.
  • Reports: when you report a post as a broker, fake or already taken, report a review, or report a couch (unsafe, not as described, money asked, or something else).

1.2 What we collect automatically

  • An account id. Thikana signs you in anonymously the first time you open it, so you can start without an account.
  • Your Google account, only if you save your account with Google: your name, email address and Google account id, from Google. We use it only to sign you back in.
  • A notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when someone answers.
  • App integrity checks (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity), to block fake apps and bots.
  • Server logs of what the app asked our servers to do (for example "intro sent"), with your account id and the time. These are for fixing problems and stopping abuse.

1.3 What we don't collect

No precise location, contacts, photo gallery, SMS, call logs, advertising id, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. There are no ads in Thikana.

2. Why we use it

  • To run Thikana: to show places that fit your office, budget and habits; to pass your card and note to the person you ask; and to share numbers when both of you say yes.
  • To keep it honest: to count society answers, to show how past tenants fared with their deposit, to hide posts that people report or that nobody has confirmed lately, and to rate-limit reports, reviews and verification emails.
  • For couch stays: to show couches near your office that take guests like you on the nights you pick; to pass your card and note to the host; to share both numbers and the address when the host says yes; and to keep hosts and guests safe (ratings, reports, and hiding a couch that is reported as unsafe until a person checks it).
  • To tell you things: for example "they said yes", "someone asked about your room", "someone asks to stay over", or "is your room still available?". If you turn on new-place alerts (Me), we compare each new post in your city with your card (gender, what you want, budget and move-in date), and keep the times of your last alerts for 24 hours so you get at most 3 a day.
  • For the Pass: to check a purchase with Google Play and turn the Pass on.
  • To meet the law: to answer lawful requests and enforce our Terms.

We rely on your consent, given when you use the app and each time you choose to share something (your number, a post, a review). You can withdraw it at any time; see section 7.

3. Who sees what

  • Anyone using Thikana in your city sees your posts: area, society, rent, rules, flatmate details, your first name and work badge. They also see society verdicts (counts and the three newest notes) and reviews, all without your name. Who answered or wrote a review is never shown.
  • Anyone using Thikana can see a couch that is taking guests: everything above except the address and the host's number. A paused couch is seen only by its host. Guests' ratings of a couch are shown on it without their name. A host's rating of a guest is never shown on its own: it only counts toward the guest's stays, average rating and late cancellations, which hosts they ask can see.
  • A host you ask to stay with sees your stay card and your note. Both numbers, and the host's address, are shared only when the host says yes. If you send your stay details to a friend from the app, you choose who gets them.
  • The person you ask to visit sees your card and your note. Your number is shared only when you both say yes. Their number is shared with you at the same time.
  • Nobody else sees your phone number, Google email, budget, which places you asked about, or where you stayed.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

4. Companies that handle data for us

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: sign-in, the database (stored in Mumbai, India, region asia-south1), photo storage, server functions, notifications and App Check. Sign-in and notifications are global Google services.
  • Google Play: Pass payments. Google handles your payment; we never see your card or UPI details. We keep a hash of the purchase token, the plan and its end date.
  • Resend (USA): sends the work-email verification code to the address you type.

Each of them processes data only to provide its service to us, under its own terms and data-protection commitments.

5. Where it's stored

Your card, posts, intros, reviews and numbers are stored in Google Cloud's Mumbai region. Sign-in, notifications and the verification email may be processed outside India by the companies above.

6. How long we keep it

  • Intros, and the numbers shared through them, are deleted 90 days after they were sent.
  • Posts that are taken or no longer confirmed are deleted 180 days after they were last confirmed, together with their photos and the reports about them.
  • Stay requests, with the numbers and address shared through them, are deleted 90 days after they were made.
  • Work-email codes expire after 15 minutes.
  • Proof-of-stay photos are deleted as soon as they're checked, and in any case after 30 days.
  • Your card, live posts, society answers, reviews and move-in records stay until you delete them or erase your account.
  • Your couch, stay ratings and stay totals stay until you erase your account. You can pause your couch at any time, which hides it from everyone, and change or remove its photos and details. A couch can't be deleted on its own, so that reports and ratings can't be wiped by listing it again.
  • Purchase records (a hash of the purchase token, the plan and its end date, but no profile data) are kept after you erase your account. This stops one purchase being used twice, and lets us meet tax and accounting rules.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

7. Your rights and choices

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • See and correct your data: your card, number, posts and reviews are all in the app (Me tab), and you can edit them there.
  • Erase everything: go to Me → Erase me. This immediately deletes your card, numbers, posts and their photos, move-in records, intros, society answers, reviews, your couch, stay requests both ways, stay ratings by or about you, reports and your sign-in account. A couch's totals (number of stays and average rating) keep counts that name nobody. Only the purchase records described above remain.
  • Withdraw consent: erase your account, turn off notifications in your phone's settings, or delete a single post or review.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or can no longer act, by writing to the Grievance Officer.
  • Complain: to our Grievance Officer (section 10), and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

If you can't open the app, see how to delete your account.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Security rules let each person read only what is theirs or what is meant to be public. Numbers are copied to an intro only after both people say yes. Sensitive actions go through our server, which checks who you are and limits how often they can be done. App Check blocks unofficial apps. No system is perfectly secure: if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

9. Age

Thikana is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that a user is under 18, we delete the account.
