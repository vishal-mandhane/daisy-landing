Delete your Thikana account

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Thikana is run by Solika Ventures Pvt Ltd. This page explains how to delete your account and the data that goes with it.

In the app

  1. Open Thikana.
  2. Go to Me, scroll to Account and tap Erase me.
  3. Confirm.

Deletion happens immediately, and it can't be undone.

Without the app

Email dayzibusiness@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Thikana account".

  • If you saved your account with Google, send the email from that Google address.
  • If you didn't, tell us the phone number you added in Thikana and the first name on your card, so we can find the account.

We will confirm within 48 hours and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your card, phone and WhatsApp numbers, notification token, posts with their photos and the reports on them, move-in records with their photos, any proof-of-stay photo still waiting to be checked, intros you sent and received (with any numbers they shared), society answers, reviews, your couch with its photos and the reports on it, stay requests you sent and received (with any numbers and address they shared), stay ratings by or about you, your stay totals, reports you made, and your sign-in account.

What is kept

  • Purchase records: a hash of a Google Play purchase token, the plan and its end date, with no profile data. They stop one purchase being used twice and meet accounting rules.
  • A couch's totals: if you stayed somewhere and rated it, the couch's number of stays and average rating still count your rating. They name nobody.
  • Server logs: for up to 30 days.
  • A hash of a work email you asked a code for: kept until its 24-hour limit ends, so the address can't be spammed with codes.
