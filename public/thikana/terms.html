1. What Thikana is, and what it isn't

Thikana is a place where tenants and owners post rooms, PGs and flats, and where people looking for a place ask to visit them. We are not a broker, an agent, a landlord or a party to any rental. We don't own, inspect or guarantee any place. We don't check every post, and we don't handle rent, deposits or agreements. Every deal is between you and the other person.

We help you judge a deal: red flags, society answers, past tenants' reviews, the deal check. These are signals from other users and simple rules, not legal or financial advice.

Never pay a token, deposit or "visit fee" before you have seen the place and met the owner or tenant in person. In Maharashtra, register the leave and licence agreement. Get the society's permission before you pay.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or over and able to make a binding contract under Indian law. Use one account, and only for yourself.

3. Your account

You start without signing up. Your account then lives only on this phone until you save it with Google (Me → Account). An account that isn't saved can't be recovered if you uninstall or change phones. A Pass can be bought only on a saved account.

You are responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else is using it.

4. Posting a place

  • Post only places you can actually show. Tell the truth about rent, deposit, charges, rules and who lives there.
  • Tenants posting a room: say honestly whether the owner has agreed to a new tenant.
  • No brokers, and no brokerage or "service fee" hidden in the charges. Post only as an owner, or as a tenant who lives there or is leaving.
  • Add details about flatmates (first name, age, work, habits) only if they have agreed.
  • Confirm your post while it's still available, and mark it taken when it's gone. Posts nobody confirms for 7 days are hidden.
  • Don't post anyone's phone number, ID or address beyond the area, society and flat number.

5. Intros and contact

When you ask to visit, the poster sees your card and your note. Numbers are shared only when both of you say yes. Use them only to arrange this place. No spam, no selling them on, no harassment. Free accounts get 3 intros a week; an intro nobody answers in 72 hours is given back.

6. Society answers and reviews

Answer about a society only if you live there or have lived there. Review only a place you actually lived in, from your own experience. Keep it about the place, the deposit and how problems were handled. No insults, no personal details about the owner or anyone else, nothing you know to be false. Reviews and answers appear without your name. We may hide or remove ones that break these rules or that people report, and we may mark reviews as "moved in through Thikana" when an intro shows that.
