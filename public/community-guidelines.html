Community Guidelines

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Dayzi exists so people can find real plans with real people. That only works if everyone feels safe enough to show up.

These Guidelines form part of our Terms of Service. Breaking them can cost you your content, your event, or your account. In serious cases we report to law enforcement.

Two things first:

  • Dayzi is 18+. No exceptions.
  • Dayzi does not organise or supervise events, and does not vet users. These rules tell you what we require of each other. They are not a promise that everyone will follow them. Use your judgement, always.

1. Be safe with each other

Never:

  • Threaten, incite, or glorify violence against anyone
  • Harass, bully, stalk, or intimidate — on Dayzi or by following someone off it
  • Share anyone's private information without their permission — home address, workplace, phone number, financial details, documents ("doxxing")
  • Share, or threaten to share, intimate images of anyone without their consent
  • Encourage anyone to harm themselves
  • Impersonate another person, brand, or organisation, or misrepresent your affiliation with one
  • Continue contacting someone who has blocked you, or evade a block with a new account

If you are in danger, call your local emergency number. Dayzi's SOS feature contacts one person you chose. It does not contact emergency services.

2. Keep it legal

Never use Dayzi to:

  • Commit, plan, promote, or facilitate any crime
  • Sell or arrange drugs, weapons, stolen goods, or other illegal items
  • Organise events that break local law, or that lack required permits, licences, or venue permission
  • Serve or encourage alcohol to anyone below the legal drinking age
  • Engage in fraud, scams, phishing, or money laundering
  • Solicit or arrange commercial sexual services
  • Traffic or exploit any person

3. Protect children — zero tolerance

Dayzi is for adults. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form, including material that is drawn, edited, or AI-generated.

Any account involved is terminated permanently and reported to the competent authorities. See our Child Safety Standards for the full policy and how to report.

If you believe a user is under 18, report them using the underage user reason.

4. No hate

Do not attack, demean, or incite hatred against people based on race, ethnicity, caste, national origin, religion, disability, disease, age, sex, gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation. Do not use slurs. Do not promote hateful ideologies or organisations.

Do not exclude people from events on those grounds. Age ranges and the practical limits of your event are fine; discrimination is not.

5. Keep content appropriate

  • No sexually explicit or pornographic content in profile photos, event images, event text, or group chats
  • No gratuitous violence or gore
  • No shock content posted to disturb people

6. Be honest about your events

  • Events must be real, and you must actually intend to hold them
  • The description, location, time, and cover image must reflect the real plan
  • Say clearly if there is a cost, if it is ticketed, or if attendees need to bring or pay for anything
  • Do not use Dayzi as a front for a sales pitch, a recruitment drive, an MLM, or an undisclosed commercial promotion
  • If you cancel, say so. People rearrange their lives around plans.

7. Be a decent guest and host

  • Turn up, or withdraw in time. Repeatedly RSVPing and ghosting affects other people and is reflected on your profile through the ghost counter.
  • Hosts: be clear about who your event is for, respond to requests, and do not accept more people than you can accommodate.
  • Guests: respect the venue, the host, and the other attendees. Leave when asked.

8. No spam or platform abuse

  • No bulk, repetitive, or unsolicited messaging
  • No bots, scrapers, or automated access
  • No harvesting other users' data
  • No attempts to break, overload, reverse-engineer, or bypass our security, moderation, or rate limits
  • No buying, selling, or transferring accounts
  • No more than one account per person
  • Do not raise a false SOS alarm

9. Reporting and blocking

Blocking — open a profile and choose Block. They can no longer interact with you.

Reporting — open a profile or press and hold a message and choose Report. Pick the closest reason; the more specific you are, the faster we can act.

Reports are reviewed by our team and are not shown to the person you reported. Filing a deliberately false report is itself a breach of these Guidelines.

We act on reports where we consider it appropriate, but we do not guarantee any particular outcome or timeframe, except where law requires one. If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services — not us.

10. What happens when rules are broken

Depending on severity and history, we may:

  1. Remove the content or cancel the event
  2. Issue a warning
  3. Limit features on your account
  4. Suspend your account temporarily
  5. Terminate your account permanently
  6. Report you to law enforcement

Some things skip straight to permanent termination and a report: child sexual abuse or exploitation, credible threats of violence, sexual assault, trafficking, and non-consensual intimate imagery.

11. Appeals

If you think we got it wrong, email developer@dayzi.live, or use the in-app appeal flow where one is offered. Tell us your username and what was actioned. A person will review it and respond within a reasonable period.

12. Changes

We may update these Guidelines as Dayzi and its risks change. Material changes will be announced in the App or on dayzi.live.
