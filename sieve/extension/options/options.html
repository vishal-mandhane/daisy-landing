Sieve

Filter any streaming feed by rating or by topic. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere you did not configure.

★ Ratings lens

Shows IMDb / Rotten Tomatoes / TMDB scores on Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, Disney+ and others, and dims or hides titles under your threshold. Bring your own free API key, and the lookups are yours; Sieve has no server.

Advanced: endpoints

Point these at a proxy if your network blocks the providers.

🎯 Topic lens

Type what you want to see (“finance, mutual funds -crypto”) or switch on presets, and everything else disappears from YouTube’s home, search, subscriptions and recommendations. Three engines, all local, best one wins:

  1. Keyword — always on. Instant, zero setup. Great for precise topics.
  2. Embeddings — a small open-source model (MiniLM, ~23 MB, downloaded once from Hugging Face and cached) understands meaning: “how I invest my salary” matches finance with no keyword in common. Runs in the browser via Transformers.js.
  3. LLM — an open-weight model such as Llama, Qwen or Gemma running in Ollama on your machine judges each batch of titles. Most accurate, needs a machine that can run it.

Privacy

Sieve has no backend and no telemetry. Settings live in your browser profile (synced by your browser if you sign in). The only network calls are the ones you configure: the ratings provider you chose, the embedding model download from huggingface.co, and the LLM endpoint you entered.
